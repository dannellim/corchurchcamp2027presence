## Confirmed

### About the camp
- Name: COR Church Camp 2027
- Church: Chapel of the Resurrection (COR), https://www.cor.org.sg
- Organised by: the El-Pizo cell group (the camp committee)
- This is a save-the-date announcement. Prices and sign-up details will be announced soon.

### Dates
- Saturday 29 May to Monday 31 May 2027
- 3 days, 2 nights
  - Day 1: Saturday, 29 May 2027
  - Day 2: Sunday, 30 May 2027
  - Day 3: Monday, 31 May 2027
- Please keep these dates free.
- Add it to your calendar: the camp website has an "Add to Google Calendar" button and an Apple / Outlook calendar file.

### Venue
- Four Points by Sheraton Desaru, a 4-star hotel
- Address: Jalan Kempas 2, Taman Desaru Utama, 81930 Bandar Penawar, Johor, Malaysia
- The hotel has an outdoor swimming pool.
- Google rating: 4.4 out of 5, from 1,171 reviews (as of 26 November 2026). Recent guests praise the breakfast at The Mesh restaurant and the warm, friendly staff.
- Directions to the hotel:
  - Google Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Four%20Points%20by%20Sheraton%20Desaru%2C%20Bandar%20Penawar
  - Apple Maps: https://maps.apple.com/?daddr=1.5762856,104.2062895&dirflg=d
  - Waze: https://waze.com/ul?ll=1.5762856,104.2062895&navigate=yes
  - The camp website also has QR codes for all three.

### Travel and camp rules
- Passport: you need your passport. We travel from Singapore to Malaysia.
- Pets: no pets at camp.

### Food and shopping near the hotel
Distances are approximate, from the hotel.
- The Mesh Restaurant: in the hotel. All-day dining with Malaysian flavours.
- Kedai Kopi Otai: about 2 minutes' walk. Local kopitiam for breakfast and kopi.
- More local eateries: 6–9 minutes' walk. Thai, Vietnamese and ayam penyet.
- SKS City Mall: a short walk from the hotel. Food court, wet market, Desaru Kopitiam, Watsons and MR DIY.
- Bandar Penawar town: supermarkets and convenience stores around the hotel, for snacks and essentials.

## Working title (not confirmed yet)

### Camp theme
- Theme: "Presence" (Chinese: 同在)
- Key verse: "My Presence will go with you, and I will give you rest." Exodus 33:14 (NIV)
- Chinese verse: "我必亲自和你同去，使你得安息。" 出埃及记 33:14（和合本）
- Tagline: Wherever our journey takes us, we remember the One who journeys with us.
- Three focuses:
  - Faith: trusting the God who goes with us
  - Love: reflecting His presence to one another
  - Community: gathering in His presence as one family
